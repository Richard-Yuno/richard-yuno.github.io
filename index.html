About YUNO

YUNO publishes New Information — short, dense briefings on how the world actually works: game theory and strategy, human nature and behaviour, the psychology of influence, luck and probability, decisions, ideas and the strange machinery of the mind. Each post draws from multiple published sources — research papers, landmark books, field studies — and refuses to end in "it depends." Every piece concludes with one truth you can act on. Curated and concluded by Richard Ngcamu. New posts land Monday to Thursday at 10:00. All coverage of persuasion and manipulation is written strictly as awareness and self-defence.
